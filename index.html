Lure Visibility Simulator

This interactive visualization models the optical transmission, backscatter, and contrast of common fishing lure colors in stained and muddy water environments. The sliders adjust the ambient light, simulating bright sun versus overcast conditions, and turbidity, representing suspended clay and dirt particles.

As turbidity increases, light transmission decays and veiling scatter increases. Dark colors like Black & Blue maintain strong silhouettes by blocking ambient light. Bright fluorescent tones like Fire Tiger and Chartreuse reflect the maximum amount of available light to retain contrast. Solid White and Bone provide stark contrast against the gloom, while natural or reflective colors like Green Pumpkin and Chrome fade rapidly into the background hue.
